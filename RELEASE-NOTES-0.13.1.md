# Portnaut 0.13.1

**Voice chat in games can use your microphone.**

Portnaut runs Windows games on Apple silicon Macs. Free and open source, by Built by Argo. Public beta.

## What's new

- **The microphone, for voice chat.** Games that use your microphone, such as voice chat in online games, got only silence: macOS never asked whether Portnaut may use the microphone, so it quietly refused. Now macOS asks the first time a game wants it. Choose **Allow**, and you can change your mind later in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone**.

## Tested so far

With FNAF Online Multiplayer's voice chat on an M4 Pro with macOS 27.2: before, macOS never asked and the game got silence; now macOS asks for the microphone when you join a lobby. Thanks to the player who reported it.

## Update

- **Have 0.13.0?** Click **Check for Updates** in Portnaut's sidebar and choose **Install Update**. This is the first update that comes through Portnaut itself.
- **Have 0.12.0 or earlier?** Download `Portnaut-0.13.1.zip` below, open it, and move **Portnaut** to your Applications folder, choosing **Replace**: your bottles, games and saves stay. Portnaut isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time, allow it in **System Settings › Privacy & Security** with **Open Anyway**.

## Privacy

Still no account, analytics or tracking. The microphone is used only by the games you play, only after you allow it, and Portnaut itself never records or sends anything.
